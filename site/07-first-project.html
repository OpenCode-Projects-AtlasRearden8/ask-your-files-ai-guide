Chapter 7 — Your First Project: From Empty Folder to First Answer

Everything so far becomes one concrete project in this chapter. Budget: one evening, zero dollars, no cloud accounts. By the end you'll have a working local pipeline you built and ran yourself, and — the important part — your first real, sourced answer about your own files.

The project

"Question clinic": take one messy real folder, and make your next question about it answerable in under a minute — with a quote you can open and show.

Pick a folder today: your insurance correspondence, your eBay returns threads, one section of notes from work. Real documents, moderate volume.

Milestone 1 — Set up the workspace (10 minutes)

mkdir -p ~/qclinic/docs ~/qclinic/builds
cp -r /path/to/your-real-folder/* ~/qclinic/docs/

Principles you're following (from Chapter 5): you copied, so originals stay untouched — this work happens on redactable copies. Everything lives under one qclinic folder — one place to back up, one place to delete.

Convert what needs converting. The quote-file tool reads .txt and .md. For PDFs, do a text export (print-to-txt in many readers, or any local desktop tool; scans with no text layer need OCR — try your OS's built-in tools first). Don't perfect this part; convert three to five core documents, move on.

Milestone 2 — Make the pile legible before any AI (15 minutes)

  1. Write ~/qclinic/docs/INDEX.md with one line per file (Chapter 2 format: date, filename, subject, tags).
  2. Write ~/qclinic/docs/OUTLINE.md: five bullets max, one per theme.

This is the step people want to skip. Do it anyway — it's 15 minutes, and it makes every later step sharper: the index is what you read when search is blurry; the outline is what the AI reads to know your pile's shape.

Milestone 3 — Build and run the quote file (10 minutes)

Python is the only requirement:

cd ~/qclinic
python3 /path/to/this-guide/tools/quote_rag.py build ./docs --store builds/quotes.json
python3 /path/to/this-guide/tools/quote_rag.py ask builds/quotes.json "your actual question" --top 5

If you don't already have Ollama, the build marks the index as TF-IDF (Chapter 3) and it still works — same commands, offline scoring.

While it runs, write down what surprises you. Most people are surprised twice: by the goods (an answer in 8 ms) and by the misses (the quote you know exists didn't score top). Misses teach the most. Nearly every miss is one of: wrong file format, a paragraph far too long, or your question's words don't appear in the document. Fix category three by re-asking with words the document itself uses — this retraining of your asking instinct is the real skill, worth more than any tool on its own.

Optional, only when Ollama is on your machine:

ollama pull nomic-embed-text
python3 tools/quote_rag.py build ./docs --store builds/quotes.json --embed nomic-embed-text

Same question again, and compare. Meaning-matching now helps when your words and the document's differ.

Milestone 4 — The first sourced AI answer (20 minutes)

Now the generation step — paste the passed quotes and your question into a chat window or a local model and ask directly:

"Here are 5 quotes I retrieved from my files, with their sources. My question: ⏎ Your question. Answer using only quotes you can see; say so for what's missing."

If you use a local model, the CLI does it:

python3 tools/quote_rag.py ask builds/quotes.json --answer "your actual question"

Now the Chapter 6 reflex: pick one non-obvious claim in the answer, open the cited file, and read the sentence it came from. This is the moment it clicks — first verified answer, fully on your machine, files untouched, nothing left your computer. Take a breath. That was the whole loop.

Milestone 5 — Name what you built (5 minutes)

You now own a pipeline with five pieces. Naming them is what makes them transferable to the other folders in your life (the book draft, the eBay ledger, the budget):

PieceWhat it isYour file
docsthe copies that get read~/qclinic/docs
INDEXone-line records, tags/INDEX.md
OUTLINEmap of themes/OUTLINE.md
buildsre-runnable indexes/builds/quotes.json
the loopsearch → quotes → answer → verifylater a habit, not a file

Add your next folder on the list the same night (just the name; setup when you get to it). Momentum comes from naming the next one now, at the exact moment it's fresh.

If something didn't work

  • "Indexed 0 quotes." No .txt/.md files in the folder — see the conversion step in Milestone 1.
  • Scores all look mushy (~0.2 everywhere). Your question probably uses words from your vocabulary, not the documents'. Re-ask with a document word ("explanation of benefits," the specific form name — actual letters).
  • The answer asserts with no quotes. You're in --answer mode without local model agreement, or the top quotes really came from something-else-but-plausible. Scroll up: the quote list is always displayed before the summary — trust the list, not the prose.
  • Everything works but feels slow. You don't need speed. The point is that it runs — even 10× "slower" beats pasting the whole library every session (Chapter 1's counting problem).
