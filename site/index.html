Ask Your Files AI Guide

A manual for asking AI questions about your own documents — fully local, fully yours.

Who this is for

You have a pile of documents. Medical bills and insurance letters. A book manuscript in progress. Buyer notes from eBay. Meeting notes from work. A shoebox of scanned paperwork. And you have an AI assistant — whatever kind, however you run it — and a feeling that it could help you ask better questions about all those files. You're right. It can.

This guide shows you how, one small honest step at a time.

What you'll learn

  • Why pasting fails at scale (Chapter 1) — and why you shouldn't feel bad about it. Everyone hits this wall.
  • Simple retrieval ideas (Chapter 2) — plain search, tagging, and outlines: the underrated 80% wins.
  • A tiny quote-file RAG (Chapter 3) — a working tool you build step by step, powered by Ollama on your own machine (with a fully-offline fallback built in).
  • The folder-packing approach (Chapter 4) — one prompt that carries your whole folder.
  • Privacy rules (Chapter 5) — how to keep personal files personal.
  • When answers deserve doubt (Chapter 6) — the healthiest skill in this whole manual.
  • A guided first project (Chapter 7) — from empty folder to first real answer, in one sitting.
  • A session checklist (Chapter 8) — a pocket version of everything important.
  • A glossary (Chapter 9) — every term in this book, explained like a friend would.

How to read it

  • If you're brand new, read Chapters 1 and 2 first. They change how you think, and they require zero tools.
  • If you want to do something today, go straight to Chapter 7 (the first project); it will send you back to earlier chapters exactly when you need them.
  • Every chapter ends with In one sentence — a plain-English takeaway you can hold in your head all day.

A promise about scope

Nothing in this guide is medical, legal, or financial advice. When we use examples like insurance letters and medical bills, we're teaching document skills, not professional judgment. Chapter 6 says more about where an AI helper's confidence is earned and where it isn't.

One line per chapter, big picture

Don't paste the library. Bring the page.

Everything after this sentence is just careful ways of doing that.

Companion tools mentioned in this guide: folder-to-prompt-cli (Chapter 4) and chat-export-archiver-cli (turning your old chat exports into a searchable archive). This site was built from Markdown with a simple local build script — python3 tools/build_site.py — so you can rebuild the whole book yourself.
