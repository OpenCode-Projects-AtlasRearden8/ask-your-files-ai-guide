Sample claim reference letter — fictional, for testing the guide's tools.

## Coverage summary

Your plan year runs February 1 through January 31. The same-day clinic visit
billed on February 11, 2024 is an in-network service under your panel year
2026 agreement. Preventive services are covered at 100 percent, and
diagnostic services carry a 60-dollar copay after the deductible.

## Why you were denied

This claim was denied under code CO-97: "the service billed is covered, but
the modifier it was billed with is inconsistent with the service."

Nothing is owed by you for this line. The billing office needs to resubmit
with a corrected modifier.
