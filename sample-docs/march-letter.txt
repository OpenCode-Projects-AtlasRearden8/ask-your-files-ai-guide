## Appeals

You may appeal within 180 days of the denial date. Send a short letter that
names the claim number, the date of service, and the reason you believe the
processing was wrong. Attach copies, not originals.

An appeal goes first to a reviewer who did not process the original claim.
Most simple corrections, like a wrong billing modifier, are fixed faster by
the billing office resubmitting than by a formal appeal.

## Good-faith estimate reminder

For scheduled services you can always ask for a good-faith estimate before
the visit. Keep estimates with the matching receipts.
